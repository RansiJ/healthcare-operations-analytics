# Executive summary

## Healthcare Operations & Performance Analytics

**Decision question.** Which hospitals warrant a closer operational or inpatient-communication review relative to eligible same-state peers?

**Evidence.** Three fixed official CMS datasets, release 2026-08-13; 2,767 acute care hospitals with emergency services in the 50 states and DC. The report uses existing run `20261031T100805Z_d4fac535`. OP_22 covers 2024; OP_18b and HCAHPS cover October 2024-September 2025. All five analytical export hashes and the SUCCESS-to-quality-report chain were verified before analysis.

**Descriptive results.**

- ED length of visit: hospital median 170 minutes (IQR 140-204 minutes); 2,632 eligible hospitals. 659 of 2,567 comparable hospitals cross the unfavorable peer-quartile threshold (25.7%).
- Left before being seen: hospital median 2% (IQR 1-3%); 2,588 eligible hospitals. 493 of 2,522 comparable hospitals cross the unfavorable peer-quartile threshold (19.5%).
- Nurses always communicated well: hospital median 78% (IQR 75-81%); 2,496 eligible hospitals. 541 of 2,430 comparable hospitals cross the unfavorable peer-quartile threshold (22.3%).

**Possible decisions.** Use eligible flags to request current workflow, arrival, staffing and survey-comment evidence. Review ED flow and inpatient communication separately. Improve evidence where source notes or sparse peer groups prevent comparison; 197 eligible hospital-measure records lack ten other peers.

**Interpretation limits.** Flags are generated by relative quartile rules and are signals to investigate, not clinical-quality diagnoses. Same-state/type peers are not matched on case mix, size, staffing or teaching status. Survey nonresponse, rounding and selection into eligibility can affect results. No causal effect, significance test, treatment recommendation or overall hospital-quality index is estimated. Hospital medians are not pooled patient medians. Databricks and Power BI are postponed.
