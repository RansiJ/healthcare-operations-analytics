# Executive summary

## Healthcare Operations & Performance Analytics

I used public CMS data to look for hospitals whose ED operations or inpatient communication scores warrant a closer review against peers in the same state. The comparisons help identify questions for an operations team to investigate; they do not establish the cause of a difference.

**Data.** The analysis covers 2,767 acute care hospitals with emergency services across the 50 states and DC, using three CMS datasets released on 2026-08-13. OP_22 covers 2024; OP_18b and HCAHPS cover October 2024-September 2025. The report reads verified exports from the completed local pipeline. [Source and file verification](evidence/report_source_verification.json).

**Descriptive results.**

- ED length of visit: hospital median 170 minutes (IQR 140-204 minutes); 2,632 eligible hospitals. 659 of 2,567 comparable hospitals cross the unfavorable peer-quartile threshold (25.7%).
- Left before being seen: hospital median 2% (IQR 1-3%); 2,588 eligible hospitals. 493 of 2,522 comparable hospitals cross the unfavorable peer-quartile threshold (19.5%).
- Nurses always communicated well: hospital median 78% (IQR 75-81%); 2,496 eligible hospitals. 541 of 2,430 comparable hospitals cross the unfavorable peer-quartile threshold (22.3%).

**Next questions.** The OP_18b shortlist in the report offers a starting point for checking current arrival patterns, staffing and throughput. Inpatient communication needs a separate review of local survey comments and communication practices. Where reporting notes or small peer groups prevent comparison, better evidence comes first; 197 eligible hospital-measure records lack ten other peers.

**Interpretation limits.** Flags are generated by relative quartile rules and are signals to investigate, not clinical-quality diagnoses. Same-state/type peers are not matched on case mix, size, staffing or teaching status. Survey nonresponse, rounding and selection into eligibility can affect results. No causal effect, significance test, treatment recommendation or overall hospital-quality index is estimated. Hospital medians are not pooled patient medians. Databricks and Power BI are postponed.
